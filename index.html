About the Lab

Understanding biodiversity through field ecology, molecular tools, and quantitative science.

The Schulte Lab integrates environmental DNA, field ecology, and quantitative analysis to better understand biodiversity and ecosystem function across aquatic and terrestrial environments. Our research develops new methods for monitoring ecological communities, evaluating environmental change, and translating scientific discoveries into practical conservation and management tools.
